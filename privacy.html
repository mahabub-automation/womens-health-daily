Privacy Policy

Women's Health Daily Uploader · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Women's Health Daily Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Women's Health Daily channels to publish their own educational videos to their own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives an OAuth access token for the owner's own YouTube channel. The token is used only to upload the owner's videos and to read the channel name to confirm the connection. The tool does not access, collect or store data about any other person.

Where data is stored

The token is stored only on the owner's private server. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The tool uses the YouTube Data API. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from Google Account permissions.
